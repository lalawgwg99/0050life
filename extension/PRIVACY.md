# Privacy Policy — 0050life Retirement Countdown

Effective: 2026-10-07

**We collect nothing.**

- All plan numbers you enter are stored only in your browser via
  `chrome.storage` (synced by Chrome to your own Google account if you
  have sync enabled — we never see it).
- The extension makes **no network requests**: no analytics, no tracking,
  no ads, no external servers.
- The "Full calculator" link opens https://0050life.pages.dev/ in a new
  tab; that website has its own privacy policy.
- We do not sell, share, or transmit any personal data, because we do
  not collect any.

Contact: via https://0050life.pages.dev/
